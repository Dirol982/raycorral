Modern - Amorphous

Modern - Amorphous

Miami Beach, Florida

Rich Forest glass mosaic traces every curve of this sculptural pool and freestanding spa. The pool’s sweeping negative edge and the spa’s free-form silhouette called for a custom tile size from our Murano Series, produced specifically to follow their fluid geometry.

Our craftsmen fitted the mosaic by hand across the curved spillover beam and changing contours, maintaining continuity where straight tile grids would interrupt the form. Manufactured and installed by Mosaicist, the custom finish makes the mosaic integral to the shape of the water.
