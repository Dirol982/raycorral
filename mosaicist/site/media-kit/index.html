Mosaicist – House of Mosaic Arts –  is a company specializing in the design, manufacture, and installation of mosaic artwork. The company combines centuries-old traditions with modern technologies, creating unique projects worldwide. 

Mosaicist was founded in the US over 25 years ago. Its history is rooted in centuries of traditional mosaic craftsmanship.
 
The company's mission is to create custom mosaic designs that blend historical and traditional techniques, particularly those inspired by the Byzantine era, with modern, high-performance installation methods. 

‍Mosaicist manages the entire project scope, from design concepts, full manufacture, to final installation for a variety of projects applications worldwide. The company specialties in exterior and underwater applications, in addition to art in public places as well as sacred artworks. Residential, commercial and government.

Ray Corral is an American artist and designer known for his work in mosaic art.

Born in Miami in 1973, Corral began his creative career in music during the 1990s, where he was a co-writer with producer Ish Ledesma and collaborated with artists such as Madonna. In 1999, he transitioned to mosaic art, a field in which he later became a prominent figure.

Corral’s work focuses on blending traditional mosaic techniques with modern design. His projects include large-scale installations for elite clients and luxury properties. Notable works in his portfolio include the largest rooftop glass mosaic mural in the United States, located at the St. Regis - Long Boat Key Resort, and a significant mosaic pool project for Home Depot co-founder Arthur Blank.

He has also been commissioned to create mosaic art for historically designated homes and is a recognized architectural city represented artist for the City of Coral Gables, FL.
