Our Process:

(services)

Design
Every commission begins with a conversation. We take time to understand the architecture, the intention, and the story a space is meant to tell — before a single sketch is drawn.
Creation
Every mosaic is set by hand, tile by tile, using techniques passed down through centuries of Byzantine tradition. Our smalti and Venetian glass are crafted in-house — over 1,000 colors, with unique shades created solely for your commission.
Installation
Our craftspeople travel to every site, managing the installation from start to finish. No detail is delegated — every work is placed with the same precision and devotion with which it was made.
Care
A Mosaicist work is made to last. We stay connected long after installation — offering guidance on preservation so that every piece continues to age with grace and intention.
