Chateau Revival - Beaux Arts

A central medallion and geometric borders give this mosaic pool the formality of a French garden. Inspired by the residence’s French and English architectural details, the composition was designed specifically for the pool, with its plumbing locations incorporated into the layout.

The precision extends beneath the visible pattern. We adjusted the artwork for the concave floor and the magnifying effect of water, helping the geometry read as a balanced plane once submerged. Designed, manufactured and installed by Mosaicist, the mosaic brings the estate’s decorative character to the water.
