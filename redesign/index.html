The story

Before founding Mosaicist, Ray Corral built a career as a professional percussionist, co-writing music throughout the 1990s while becoming a partner in Mecca Multi Media (M3), one of the Southeast’s leading recording studios.

By the end of the decade, music gave way to mosaic. Since then, it has remained the focus of his life’s work.
