Ray Corral is recognized as one of the leading figures in architectural mosaic art.

About

Since founding the studio in 1999, he has led Mosaicist in completing more than 650 custom mosaic commissions for private residences, luxury resorts, and architectural projects around the world.

The studio’s work is rooted in the centuries-old tradition of hand-cut glass mosaics, adapted for contemporary architecture and underwater environments.

Mosaicist

Ray Corral founded Mosaicist in 1999.

He studied under master mosaicists Luigi Scodeller and Giuseppe Semeraro, later teaching at Scuola Mosaicisti del Friuli in Italy. His work combines traditional hand-cut glass techniques with contemporary architectural design and engineering.

Over the past twenty-five years, Mosaicist — House of Mosaic Arts has completed more than 650 bespoke mosaic commissions under Ray Corral’s creative direction.

The Mosaicist’s portfolio includes the largest hand-cut glass rooftop mosaic in the United States, comprising more than 1.3 million individually placed tesserae, the country’s largest hand-cut mosaic swimming pool, and commissions for private residences, luxury resorts, and public spaces across the United States and internationally.

Public service

Ray Corral serves on the Board of the Lowe Art Museum at the University of Miami and the Coral Gables Art Advisory Board.

Today

Today, Corral continues to lead Mosaicist — House of Mosaic Arts, creating architectural mosaic commissions around the world while supporting artists through philanthropic initiatives and cultural organizations.
