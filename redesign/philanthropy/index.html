Philanthropy

Art has shaped Ray Corral’s career. Supporting the people who create it has become an equally important part of his work.

In 2023, Ray Corral and Catherine Cathers established the Mosaic Art Fund through the Coral Gables Community Foundation.

The mission

The fund was created with a straightforward mission: to provide direct financial support to working visual artists.

Unlike many grant programs, the fund focuses exclusively on professional artists rather than institutions or academic programs.

The fund supports
  1. New artistic projects01
  2. Skill development and professional growth02
  3. Exhibitions and public visibility03
  4. Studio expenses and production costs04
  5. Artist residencies05

Beyond the fund

In addition to the Mosaic Art Fund, Corral remains actively involved in the cultural life of South Florida through his work with the Lowe Art Museum and the Coral Gables Art Advisory Board, supporting public art initiatives and the preservation of artistic craftsmanship.
