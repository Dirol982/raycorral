Mediterranean Revival - Oceanic

An ocean-inspired composition unfolds across the floor of this custom mosaic pool. Marine imagery and flowing ornament stand out against deep blue glass, creating an under water artwork whose full scale becomes visible from the terraces above.

We developed the design around the owner’s love of the sea, tailoring its proportions to the pool and the surrounding Mediterranean Revival architecture. Designed, manufactured and installed by Mosaicist, the mosaic turns the water into the courtyard’s visual centerpiece, with intricate details that invite a closer look.
