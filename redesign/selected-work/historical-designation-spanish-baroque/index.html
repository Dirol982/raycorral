Selected work · No. 06 of 08

Historical DesignationSpanish Baroque

An original Art Deco composition brings the character of this historic residence into its courtyard pool. A detailed central motif, framed borders and a blue glass mosaic field give the water a jewel-like depth, with 24-karat white and yellow gold accents adding points of light.

The design takes its cues from the estate’s existing Art Deco reliefs, translating their shapes into artwork made specifically for the pool. Scaled to the courtyard and its surrounding architecture, the mosaic gives the renovated aquatic space a distinctive identity rooted in the home’s original details.
